Store Page Check

A free, open-source bookmarklet that reads the Shopify store page you are on and lists what to look at before a Google Merchant Center review.

Get the bookmarklet

On a computer, drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

Store Page Check

Then open a page of your store (a product page gives the most results) and click the bookmark. A small panel appears at the bottom right. Click the x (or press Esc) to close it.

What it checks

The page you are on, plus the store's own policy pages. Each item gets one of three results:

OK looks present   Check look at this yourself   N/A does not apply here, or could not be checked

  1. Policy links. Is there a link to a refund/return policy, shipping policy, privacy policy, terms of service and a contact page? Shopify's default paths (such as /policies/refund-policy and /pages/contact) are recognized.
  2. Policy page contents. It also opens the refund, shipping, privacy and terms pages from the same site (read only) and notes whether a page returns an error, has little or no text in its HTML, or still contains template text such as [LINK TO REFUND POLICY]. Text that an embed or script adds after the page opens (for example from Termly) is not seen; for known embed services the item shows N/A instead.
  3. Contact details. Are an email address and a phone number shown on this page? Does any email link point to an address that cannot work (for example, one with two @ signs or quote marks around it), does an address end in a typo such as .ccom, or is a template's sample address such as support@example.com, a sample phone number such as (123) 456-7890, or a social link to Shopify's own account such as twitter.com/shopify still showing? The business address is not judged; the panel only reminds you to check it yourself.
  4. Product data. On a product page, it reads the Product structured data, in JSON-LD and/or microdata (itemtype schema.org/Product), and looks for name, image, price, currency, availability, brand, an identifier (gtin, mpn or sku), shippingDetails and hasMerchantReturnPolicy. Several offers or an AggregateOffer are handled. The panel says which format was found; RDFa is not read.
  5. Price match. If both a product data price and an og:price:amount meta tag exist, are they the same?
  6. Image alt text. How many images have a missing or empty alt attribute (decorative images marked role="presentation" or aria-hidden are left out)?

Every result has a one-line reason and a link to the related Google documentation: Misrepresentation policy, Editorial and professional requirements, Merchant listing structured data, Google Images best practices.

How to use it

Computer

  1. Show your browser's bookmarks bar.
  2. Drag the Store Page Check button above onto the bar.
  3. Open one of your store pages and click the bookmark.

Phone or tablet (manual bookmark)

Phones cannot drag links, so make the bookmark by hand. Support differs between mobile browsers, and this has not been tested on all of them.

  1. Tap into the box below and copy all of the text.
  2. Bookmark any page in your browser, then edit that bookmark.
  3. Rename it "Store Page Check" and replace its address with the copied text.
  4. Open your store page and open the bookmark from your bookmarks list.

If your mobile browser will not run bookmarklets, use a computer instead.

Some stores use a security setting that blocks bookmarklets from adding the panel. If nothing appears, that may be the reason.

Where to fix things in Shopify

Menu names change between Shopify admin versions. If you cannot find an item, use the search box at the top of the admin.

Policy pages

  • Write or edit the refund, shipping, privacy and terms texts in Settings > Policies.
  • Make them easy to find: add links in the menu shown in your store's footer. In the admin this menu editor is called Menus or Navigation, depending on the version.
  • A contact page is a normal page you create in Online Store > Pages, then link in the same footer menu.
  • The checker also opens each policy page (read only, same site) to see whether its HTML has real text or only a heading (text added later by an embed or script is not seen), and whether template text such as [LINK TO REFUND POLICY] or [STORE NAME] is still in it. Replace any such text with your own.

Email and phone

  • Your store's contact details are in Settings > Store details. Check what customers actually see on the contact page and in the footer, because the theme decides where they appear.
  • Your business address: check that it is shown somewhere customers can find it, and that it matches what is in your Merchant Center account.

Product data

Open the product from Products in the admin.

  • name, image, price: title, media and Pricing fields on the product page.
  • brand: many themes use the product's Vendor field for brand. If brand is missing, check that field first.
  • identifier: the Inventory area of the product (or of each variant) has SKU and Barcode fields. Use the real barcode (GTIN/UPC/EAN) if the product has one.
  • availability and currency: these come from your inventory settings and your store currency. Which markup your theme prints is decided by the theme, so if a value looks wrong in the panel but right in the admin, ask the theme's developer.
  • shippingDetails and hasMerchantReturnPolicy: many themes do not print these. Shipping and return information can also be set inside Google Merchant Center. Pick one place and keep it consistent with your store's policy pages.

Price mismatch

  • Compare the price in the product's Pricing section with the price shown on the page. If an app or discount changes prices on the page, check how it works with your product data.
  • If the two values in the panel differ, the markup is coming from the theme or an app. Ask whoever maintains it.

Image alt text

  • On a product, click an image in the media area and choose the option to add alt text.
  • For images placed in theme sections, use the theme editor (Online Store > Themes > Customize) and look for an alt text field in that section, if the theme provides one.

Privacy

The checker runs inside your browser on the page you click it on. The checker itself has no analytics or tracking. Besides reading that page, it loads the store's own policy pages (refund, shipping, privacy, terms) from the same site with ordinary read-only requests, and it sends nothing to the author or anyone else. (Scripts the store page already runs, such as its own analytics, keep running as usual.) This page uses no external fonts or styles. Its only external script is Cloudflare Web Analytics, which, per Cloudflare, counts visits to this page without cookies or tracking of individual visitors; Cloudflare receives visitors' IP addresses but says it does not store them in core databases or logs. The checker itself does not load it. The code is a single readable file, checker.js.

The optional feedback form is a Google Form, so answers are stored in my Google account. Your email is asked for only if you agree to be contacted. I use it only to reply about this checker, never share or sell it, and delete it when you ask; I delete all form answers by 2027-04-30 at the latest. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard visit logs such as IP addresses.
